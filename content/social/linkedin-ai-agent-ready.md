# LinkedIn post: Is your business AI-agent ready?

Most companies asking about AI agents start with the model.

That is usually the wrong place to start.

Before an agent can do useful work, the business needs one workflow that is ready for it:

→ A specific trigger and outcome  
→ Approved sources of truth  
→ Limited tool permissions  
→ Human approval for sensitive actions  
→ Safe failure and recovery paths  
→ Evaluations based on real tasks  
→ Logs, alerts, and named owners  
→ A business result you can measure

If those pieces are missing, an agent will not repair the workflow. It may only make the failure move faster.

The best first AI agent is rarely the most ambitious one.

It is the smallest controlled workflow where better speed, consistency, or visibility creates a result the business can verify.

## Short alternate version

Is your business AI-agent ready?

The answer has less to do with the model than most teams expect.

A production agent needs a defined workflow, approved data, narrow permissions, human approval boundaries, safe recovery, evaluations, monitoring, and clear ownership.

Without those pieces, AI does not fix a broken process. It makes the failure move faster.

Start with one narrow workflow and one measurable outcome. Earn the right to expand from there.
