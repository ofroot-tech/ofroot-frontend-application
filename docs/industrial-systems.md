# Industrial Systems positioning guardrail

## Purpose

Industrial Systems extends OfRoot Technology's software engineering, AI, and automation work into complex physical and field environments. It creates a credible bridge from digital systems to future critical-infrastructure and secure-systems work without presenting OfRoot as a defense contractor.

## Positioning

The central idea is **software for the physical world**. OfRoot may describe building software that connects field operations, physical assets, operational data, AI, and enterprise systems.

The capability progression is:

1. Software engineering
2. AI and automation
3. Industrial systems
4. Secure and autonomous systems
5. Future defense technology

The current public position stops at industrial systems. Later stages require proof before public claims.

## Claims

OfRoot may say that, through direct B2B software consulting engagements, it has contributed engineering expertise to technology supporting complex industrial environments. Public capability language may include computer vision workflows, OCR-assisted identification, edge processing, human verification, offline-resilient capture, structured operational records, enterprise API integrations, operational notifications, and AI-assisted information retrieval.

Capability statements must not imply that every capability has been commercially deployed. Engagement language must distinguish OfRoot's engineering contribution from ownership of an entire client project.

## Confidentiality

Never publish confidential customer names, customer logos without permission, facility names or locations, site imagery, private screenshots, infrastructure diagrams, project values, revenue attribution, critical-infrastructure datasets, testimonials, certifications, government relationships, or deployment claims that cannot be supported by public evidence.

Do not claim that OfRoot manufactured hardware, drones, sensors, cameras, or robotics. Do not imply that OfRoot holds Department of Defense contracts or currently operates as a defense contractor.

## Future expansion

The software foundation may develop toward critical infrastructure, secure systems, cameras, drones, robotics, sensor fusion, spatial computing, and autonomous inspection. These should remain future-facing architecture directions until evidence supports stronger language.

The intended path is:

Industrial Systems → Critical Infrastructure → Secure and Autonomous Systems → Government and Defense
